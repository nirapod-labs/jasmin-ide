# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about the code, not the person who wrote it. Assume
the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- **Be direct and be kind.** Say what is wrong with a change and why, and say
  it about the change. A question that seems obvious is still worth answering.
- **Take review as help.** A reviewer who finds a real problem has done you a
  favour. A reviewer who is wrong is still worth a plain answer.
- **Give review as help.** Say what you would change and what you would keep.
  Point at the line, not at the author.
- **Accept that people differ.** Experience, language, time zone, health and
  what someone has going on at home are all invisible. None of them is a fair
  target.
- **Respect the process.** A decision that took a discussion is not reopened
  because one person did not like it.
